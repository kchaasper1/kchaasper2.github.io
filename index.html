Phase 1: pure logic, no graphics (start here) 1. config.js: all tunable numbers (multipliers, costs, salaries, starting loadout) check 2. rng.js: seeded random generator check 3. unitTypes.js: unit type data (damage range, authority cost, hire cost, salary) check 4. progression.js: per-type levels and fixed-point XP check 5. formation.js: square and line layouts, exposed units, casualty and reserve rules check 6. army.js: armies, stacks, merge/split, authority checks, formation switching check 7. battleRules.js: facing, flank bonuses, counterattack, shared roll, XP award check Phase 2: battle (the vertical slice) 8. battleGrid.js: grid, movement, placement by attack direction check 9. battleAI.js: AI for enemies and disconnect takeover check 10. battleUI.js: rendering, unit-count numbers, controls check Phase 3: overworld 11. overworld.js: free movement, facing, contact detection check 12. economy.js: gold, hiring, salaries, desertion check 13. villages.js: patronage, capture, hiring points 14. monsters.js: roaming monsters and spawning 15. mapGenerator.js: procedural maps (World Battle) 16. campaignMaps.js: hand-made maps and missions 17. turnResolver.js: simultaneous turn resolution Phase 4: online and menus 18. firebase.js: setup for the new project 19. auth.js: anonymous start, Google upgrade 20. saveData.js: unlockables and campaign progress 21. matchSync.js: PvP match records, orders, replay, presence, reconnect 22. menu.js: main menu and mode select 23. main.js: wires everything together
